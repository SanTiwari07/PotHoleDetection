Sensor node wiring

ESP32 DevKit V1 with the MPU6050, DS3231 RTC and NEO-6M GPS. The I²C devices share one bus with 4.7 kΩ pull-ups.

3.3 V power GND SDA · GPIO 21 SCL · GPIO 22 GPS TX → RX2 · GPIO 16 TX2 → GPS RX · GPIO 17
